Welcome to North Star Bakery

Good Morning, Neighbor

Every day starts before sunrise at North Star Bakery. Our bakers shape each loaf by hand, pull trays of pastries from the oven while the street is still quiet.

Whether you are grabbing a croissant on the way to work, picking up a loaf for family dinner, or planning desserts for a big event, we are glad you chose us.

About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began in 2014 as a single oven and a folding table at the Saturday farmers market. Neighbors kept coming back for the sourdough, and two years later we opened our shop on Maple Street.

We named the bakery after the North Star because it has always helped people find their way home. We hope our shop feels the same way: a warm, familiar place where you are always welcome, whether you stop by every morning or once a year for a birthday cake.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Good bread starts with good ingredients, and we do our best to do right by you.

  • Flour: Wheat and rye from a family mill two hours away.
  • Dairy and eggs: butter, milk, and eggs from local farms.
  • Fruit: seasonal fruit from the same farmers market where we started.
  • Nothing artificial: no preservatives.

Any bread left at the end of the day is donated to a neighborhood food pantry, so nothing goes to waste.

Meet Our Team

Charlie Smith, Founder and Head Baker

Charlie learned to bake bread in her grandmother's kitchen and has been tending the same sourdough starter for over ten years. She still shapes the first loaves every morning.

Ed Burger, Pastry Chef

Ed is the reason our croissants have so many layers. He also designs our custom celebration cakes and loves a challenging request.

Charlotte Web, Front of House and Events

Charlotte knows most of our regulars by name. She helps families and event planners choose the right treats and makes sure every pre-order is ready on time.
